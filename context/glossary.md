# Glossary

FuelPass team words, one line each. The morning brief proposes new entries under "Words you may not know"; you confirm one with `term: X = ...` in a Slack message to yourself.

- **SP / story points**: effort estimate on a Jira story. Velocity baseline 200 to 250 SP per two-week sprint.
- **RICE**: scoring for discovery ideas: Reach x Impact x Confidence / Effort. Lives in Jira project FUEL.
- **PM review**: Jira status. Engineering says done; waiting for product to accept. 2-working-day rule. The ticket's assignee chases it.
- **carryover** (Jira label): work that was already in QA, PM review or Ready for release when the sprint started. In the sprint, but left out of velocity and the target comparison.
- **Ready for release**: Jira status. Accepted by product, waiting for the release. Counts as "sprint done".
- **Refinement**: Thursday meeting where stories are explained and estimated. Feeds "Ready for development".
- **Pre-refinement (stories review)**: Tuesday session where stories are walked through before refinement.
- **Definition of Ready**: checklist a story must pass before estimation (Confluence page 87588865).
- **Sprint review**: sprint-end Monday, on staging: finished work is shown, spill-over is agreed; doubles as the go-live sign-off.
- **Retrospective**: sprint-end Monday workshop on how the team works.
- **Staging / production**: test environment / live environment.
- **Staging cut-off**: sprint code is on staging by Friday morning of sprint week 2; smoke tests run that Friday.
- **Feature flag trio**: one engineer, one product person and QA decide what goes live behind a flag at each release.
- **Confluence brief**: the page the product owner writes when an idea enters Discovery, so design can start from it.
- **KYC / KYB**: identity checks on a person / a company before full access.
- **Gemini notes**: the meeting notes Google Meet writes (summary, next steps, transcript), sent by email and attached to the calendar event.
