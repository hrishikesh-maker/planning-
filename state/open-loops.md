# Open loops

Commitments the routines track until closed. One line per item. "Mine" means you owe it. "Waiting" means someone owes you. Closed items move to the bottom with the date.

Format: `- [status] YYYY-MM-DD | owner | what | source link | due (if said)`

This file lists things to check, not proof that they are still open. Notes such as "still unanswered" are what an earlier run saw that day. The morning brief checks every item again before calling it open (skill step 3G).
