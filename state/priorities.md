# Current week priorities

How this file is kept: the week planner writes a "proposed" section every Monday. If you have a team planning meeting, the next morning brief writes a "decided" section above it with the items the team agreed that you own or wait on. The morning brief also corrects the plan when you post or edit your "My week" message. When a "decided" section exists, it is the plan of record; the proposal stays below it for comparison. Sections older than four weeks are deleted.
